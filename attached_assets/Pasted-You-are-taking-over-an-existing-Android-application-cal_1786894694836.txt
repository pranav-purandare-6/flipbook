PART 30 — WHAT THE PAGE CURL MUST LOOK LIKE
============================================================

Imagine a physical hardcover book.

The current page is a sheet of paper.

When the user drags from the right:

The right side of the paper lifts.

The page bends toward the center.

The fold moves across the page.

The back side of the paper becomes visible.

A curved shadow appears under the folded sheet.

The page progressively covers/reveals the underlying page.

The page follows the finger.

When released:

The page either:

A) completes the turn

or

B) falls back into its original position.

The final animation must look like physical paper.

PART 33 — FORWARD PAGE TURN
============================================================

Forward turn:

User drags from right → left.

The page curls from the right edge.

The folded portion progresses toward the left.

The next page remains underneath.

The current page progressively disappears behind the curl.

The shadow moves with the folded page.

When completed:

new page becomes current.
